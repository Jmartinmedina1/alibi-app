## Full description (English)

Everyone was at the same secret place last night… except one of you.

ALIBI is a quick, funny party game of bluffing and deduction for 3–8 players. Everyone gets the secret place, except the Liar, who only gets a vague hint. Take turns grilling each other with silly questions, tap the answer that proves you were there, and point at the Liar before they slip away.

• No typing, no freezing up: pick from ready-made answers, so kids, shy players and Spanish learners can bluff too.
• Play with friends on your own phones: share a 4-letter room code or link. Friends join from any phone browser, no download needed.
• Practice against bots any time, with tips for your first games.
• OBJECTION! Get accused and defend yourself with an absurd excuse.
• Joker mode for extra-ridiculous answers. Easy, Medium and Hard.
• Español pack: 20 places across the Spanish-speaking world. Hold any line to see it in English.
• 46 places, from roller rinks to Rapa Nui, with a fun fact after every case.

Free. No account. No ads. No in-app purchases.
