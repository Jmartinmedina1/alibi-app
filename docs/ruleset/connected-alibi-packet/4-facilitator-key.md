**The Liar is Chef Bruno.** He has **only two substantive alibi choices** (A and B). C and D on his card are Review slots, not answers. This paper test does not yet satisfy the intended four-answer game. Dot and Leo are honest. Bruno was in the judges' tent from 9:12 to 9:25.

## What each investigation establishes

| Investigation | If Bruno locked A | If Bruno locked B |
|---|---|---|
| Record A (register) | Doesn't decide anything: restocking makes no sales | Doesn't decide anything: consistent with being away |
| Record B (raffle sheet) | Confirms every clause of Dot's alibi, so Dot is honest. Also confirms Bruno's true handoff clause, which doesn't clear him. | Same; and if Dot's answer is already known, Bruno is exposed |
| Record C (Food-truck ice delivery — 9:18) | **Exposes Bruno:** the record shows the truck **padlocked** at 9:18; Bruno says he **kept it open** from 9:10 to 9:30. Open vs padlocked, nothing more is inferred | Doesn't decide anything: consistent with being away |
| Record D (stage sheet) | Confirms one clause of Leo's alibi only | Same |
| Ask Dot | Doesn't decide anything | **Conflict:** "none to Bruno" vs "bought tickets", so Dot or Bruno is lying |
| Ask Leo | Doesn't decide anything | Doesn't decide anything |
| Ask Bruno | His answer repeats his alibi | Same |

## Reference playthroughs

- **Bruno locked B. Ask Dot, then open the raffle sheet.**
  1. Dot and Bruno conflict.
  2. The sheet confirms all of Dot's alibi.
  3. Accuse Bruno: **Detectives win.**
- **Bruno locked A. Open the raffle sheet, then the register.**
  1. Dot is confirmed.
  2. The register can't tell restocking from absence.
  3. Choose D, insufficient evidence: **draw.**
  - Opening "Food-truck ice delivery — 9:18" instead of the register exposes Bruno: open vs padlocked at 9:18.

## Known limits

- **One-step exposure.** If opened, "Food-truck ice delivery — 9:18" exposes option A in one step. Its public title names the food truck and 9:18, so it is an obvious check against option A.
- **Fixed Liar.** Bruno is always the Liar in this packet, so a second play is solved.
- **Leo's skate demo has no record.**
- **Option B is exposed by one natural question plus one record.** It may prove the weaker cover. Untested with players.
