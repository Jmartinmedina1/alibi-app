# The Missing Trophy: player packet

## Briefing

At 9:20 the baking trophy went missing from the judges' tent. Three suspects say where they were from **9:05 to 9:30**.

**The rule:** exactly one suspect has a made-up alibi. The other two are telling the truth, and their answers are accurate.

**Your job:** find the made-up alibi. You don't need to prove who took the trophy.

- **Two investigations.** Each is one record **or** one question, in any order.
- **Every statement is locked.** Nobody can change what they said.

## Locked accounts

- **Granny Dot:** "I collected the raffle box from Bruno at his truck at 9:05, then sold raffle tickets at the raffle table from 9:10 to 9:30."
- **Skater Leo:** "I did the skate demo until 9:12, signed out the microphone at 9:15, and was at the raffle table from 9:18 for the draw."
- **Chef Bruno:** *(the facilitator hands you Bruno's account card)*

## Your turn (choose A, B, C or D)

- **A. Open a record** (each can be opened once):
  - A: Food-truck register
  - B: Raffle committee sheet
  - C: Ice vendor's delivery log
  - D: Stage manager's sign-out sheet
- **B. Ask a suspect** "Who did you see, and what did you do, between 9:10 and 9:30?":
  - A: Granny Dot
  - B: Skater Leo
  - C: Chef Bruno
  - D: Back
- **C. Review** the accounts and anything revealed so far.
- **D. Accuse now.**

## Accusation (choose A, B, C or D)

- **A:** Granny Dot
- **B:** Skater Leo
- **C:** Chef Bruno
- **D:** Insufficient evidence

**Outcomes (fixed):**
- **You name the made-up alibi:** the Detectives win.
- **You name an honest suspect:** the Liar wins.
- **D:** a draw. Nobody wins.
